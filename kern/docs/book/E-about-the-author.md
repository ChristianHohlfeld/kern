# About the Author

![Christian Heinrich Hohlfeld](../buch/bilder/autor.jpg)

**Christian Heinrich Hohlfeld** is a software engineer in Konstanz, Germany. He has been programming
since 2010 – in C++, Java, C#/.NET, TypeScript and Python – and holds a B.Sc. in Software Engineering
from HTWG Konstanz. His approach: first understand how the work is really done, then build the whole
system until it is live. Routine can go to software; decisions stay with people.

Games and 3D have been with him from the start. His Android app MMA News, built and published on his
own from 2010, reached around 220,000 downloads. In 2015 he released Alien Star Racing, a game with
procedurally generated levels on his own toolkit on top of cocos2d-x (C++); it placed in the top ten
of a Microsoft-sponsored contest. With VoxelShaper he is building a voxel CAD for 3D printing in the
browser, with kern3d a dependency-free WebGL core – the forerunner of Kern.

Professionally he has built software for billing and financing (ines GmbH), a crafts platform
(wirsindhandwerk.de) and, as a freelancer, for clients including Robert Bosch GmbH. Today he is the
advisor for digital transformation and process optimisation and the internal data protection officer
at WOBAK, the municipal housing company of Konstanz, where he leads the digitalisation project group
and the internal AI pilot group. Alongside, he builds and runs his own products, among them
digitalisierungsplanung.de, a tool that records real work in the browser and makes it available to
AI agents through an MCP server.

## How Kern and this book were made

Kern and this book were made with artificial intelligence, and nobody should have to read that
between the lines. Most of the source code and the text was written by an AI system (Claude by
Anthropic), under the author's direction.

**What lies with the author:**

- **The idea and the goal:** an engine in pure C++ that runs in the browser on phones and desktops
  equally fast and looking the same, with an editor, a robot simulation and its own training system
  for neural networks.
- **The architecture and the choice of components:** C++20 to WebAssembly and WebGL2, Box3D for
  physics, the Micro Duck with Pollen Robotics' original networks instead of rebuilt motions,
  fidelity to the real robot over tricks of its own, the licensing model.
- **The standard and the decisions:** what is good enough, what is thrown away and what goes live.
  He tested every feature on real devices; many improvements came out of that testing.
- **The responsibility** for the software and the book.

**What lies with the AI:** turning it into code and tests, research in sources and papers, writing
and translating the chapters – at a pace no single person reaches without such tools.

The result shows what is possible today: an experienced developer who knows what he wants to build
and makes the right decisions builds with AI, in a short time, what used to take a team. The AI
writes; what gets built, and whether it is any good, is decided by a human.
